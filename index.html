Get your Client ID
  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in (Spotify requires Premium).
  2. Click Create app. Any name works.
  3. Under Redirect URIs, add this exact address:
  4. Tick Web API, accept, and Save.
  5. Open Settings and copy the Client ID.

    Get your listening history (free)
    1. Go to spotify.com → Account → Privacy settings.
    2. Under "Download your data", request Extended streaming history. It arrives by email in a few days (sometimes longer).
    3. Unzip it and choose the Streaming_History_Audio_….json files below.
